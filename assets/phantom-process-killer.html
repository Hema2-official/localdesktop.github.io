Keep Android from stopping Linux programs

Since Android 12, a phantom process killer ends apps' background processes when there are more than 32 of them across all apps, or when one of them uses a lot of CPU. Programs on Local Desktop can spawn a lot of these, which means that anything could stop suddenly without warning.

Turn it off once with any of these. It stays off until you turn it back on.

On this phone

In Developer options, turn on Disable child process restrictions.

Open Developer options

No Developer options? Tap Build number seven times in Settings › About phone › Software information.
