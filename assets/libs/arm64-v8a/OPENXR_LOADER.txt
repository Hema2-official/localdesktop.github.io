The Khronos OpenXR loader for Android, used by immersive mode on VR headsets (src/android/xr.rs).
Taken unchanged from jni/arm64-v8a/ of the official AAR on Maven Central:
https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/org/khronos/openxr/openxr_loader_for_android/1.1.63/openxr_loader_for_android-1.1.63.aar
(AAR sha1 bb72fea1e7bdd35b381ae2a705206d912a1d5b30, as published next to it)
License: Apache-2.0 OR MIT (Copyright The Khronos Group Inc.)

The AAR's AndroidManifest.xml asks apps for the OPENXR and OPENXR_SYSTEM permissions and for
<queries> of the runtime brokers and the runtime and API layer services; manifest.yaml has them.

Primary file:
assets/libs/arm64-v8a/libopenxr_loader.so
